Human-in-the-Loop Workflow

Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Checkpoint

You maintain ultimate control over your vacation. Before finalizing the booking blueprint, the graph halts via LangGraph `interrupt()`, presenting an interactive draft for your review and custom instructions.

  • Stateful thread suspension: Graph state saved persistently in PostgreSQL.
  • Flexible revision feedback: Request adjustments or approve instantly.
  • Zero unwanted automation: No finalized plan without human consent.
Specialist Specifications
Interrupt Primitive LangGraph interrupt()
Resume Primitive travel_graph.invoke(Command(resume=...))
Database Engine External PostgreSQL (Render SSL)
Human Autonomy 100% Guaranteed Control
